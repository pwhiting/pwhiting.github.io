Our bed-and-breakfast last night was wonderful. The amount of food they had in their kitchen for us to make breakfast was outstanding. We barely touched what was available and yet we ate a lot. It was a fun place to stay. We’d like to go back to that one again.

We knew we had a bit more miles today so we left by 9 AM. Much of the trail was in good condition until we were about half way through our ride - then we hit the section the trail crew is working on. Once again they went out of their way to let us by. There are other places we’ve ridden where such construction would cause the work crew to just close the trail. We’re so glad they kept it open, as there wasn’t a reasonable option to bypass it on roads. By October, the trial should be improved all the way to Paw Paw tunnel.

The tunnel had been on our mind much of the day, similar to the detour the day before. The tunnel is a pretty rough trail, barely wide enough for one bike. Gratefully there is a railing along the side that faces the canal. The canal sits about 10 feet below the trail. We of course didn’t want to test the sturdiness of the railing by hitting it, so did our best to stay in the middle. Three times we met other bikers going the opposite direction and had to carefully get around them. The tunnel is 3000 feet long. It was quite costly both in terms of money and lives to build. Again, we are grateful that Someone had the foresight to preserve it once the canal was no longer in use. The alternative to the tunnel is a 2 mile steep trail that would not accommodate our tandem bike.
