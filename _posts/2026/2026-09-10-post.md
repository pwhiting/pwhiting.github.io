Since we had a shorter ride planned for today and since we couldn’t get into our new Airbnb until three, we had a pretty relaxing morning. We ate breakfast in an old church converted into a café. It was called Beans in the Belfry. It was a fun start.

![Beans in the Belfry](https://photos.app.goo.gl/NrbURxpYo5AjG5i87)

Our ride today was peaceful. I think I’ve hit the point mentally where I can pedal and not think about Work or other things back home. Natalie and I mostly talked about every random little thing we could think of. When it’s been silent for a while, one of us will say What are you currently thinking about?” and we have a new topic to discuss.

![Butterfly along the trail](https://photos.app.goo.gl/Kt9gwWKB45GoQnQH6)

Today we saw lots of turtles, sunning themselves on logs. When we stop to take a picture, they would all dive into the water.

![Nice view of the Potomac](https://photos.app.goo.gl/Rqbm3NMypH15ryYi7)

We arrived at our very nice Airbnb around 3:30. It has a swimming pool, although we might not use it. It’s in a super fancy neighborhood with multi-acre lots. We are in a nicely decorated loft apartment that was built onto the garage. It is by far the nicest place. We are going to stay on this trip. We stay here through Monday morning.

![View from our porch](https://photos.app.goo.gl/Wb6h6ViwHrnfpB8V6)

We unloaded our Pioneers and rode our bike about 2 miles to a grocery store. First, we stopped by Chipotle and ate a ton of food. I had four refills of lemonade. It was a good thing we ate and rehydrated before going into Safeway to buy food for the rest of the week. It is surprising how much we take for granted when we have cars. I loaded up a shopping cart nearly to the top, and then we had to fit all that food into our four panniers and ride home. We now have about 4 days’ worth of groceries here with us.

Tomorrow we ride about 15 miles to the start of the C&O Canal Trail. We may try to go into downtown DC too, depending on the crowds.
