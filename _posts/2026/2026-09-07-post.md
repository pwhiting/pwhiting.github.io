We will make a real post and update this entry tomorrow.

We’ve left at about 9:30 AM and rode until about 7 PM. We had a good ride, long slow uphill for 20 miles followed by 30 miles of downhill to finish the GAP. This put us in Cumberland, MD. We thought about staying in a hotel there, but wanted to get more miles as the weather was good and we felt rested after that long descent (which will be a piece of work on the way back to Pittsburgh).

We rode about 20 miles on the C&O Canal Trail and set up camp in a hiker/biker campsite off the trail. These sites have no other access than the trail, so they are quiet. We are the only people in ours. 

We set up camp, pumped and filtered water, made dinner (for dinner, we had taco soup Bryan had freeze dried it before our trip — it was delicious), and headed to bed.

It has been a beautiful sunshiny day with perfect temperature.

We went through several tunnels today and across a long viaduct, and over several rail bridges. Read about “rail banking” to understand how these Rails to Trails came to be. We are so grateful that they were able to make good use of the railroad right-of-way. There would have been no other way to afford to make these trails.

We are grateful.
